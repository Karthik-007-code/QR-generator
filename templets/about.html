About QR_gen

QR_gen is an intuitive web application built with FastAPI and modern web standards. It enables individuals and business professionals to effortlessly create, customize, preview, and export high-resolution QR codes. Whether you need QR codes for website URLs, contact details, Wi-Fi credentials, or text messages, QR_gen provides a seamless workflow.

Key Features

QR Code Generation

Generate crisp, customizable QR codes instantly with real-time live preview rendering.

User Authentication

Secure account sign-up and login powered by password hashing and HTTP-only JWT sessions.

User Profile & Session

Interactive profile popover in top navigation bar displaying logged-in credentials and status.

Multiple Export Formats

Download generated QR codes as PNG or vector SVG files, or copy directly to your clipboard.

Generation History

Access previously generated QR codes during your active session for quick re-downloading.

Custom styling

Adjust foreground and background colors to craft QR codes tailored to your brand identity.
